🧭 Roadmap

TL;DR: psanctuarychurch is the church's professional face on GitHub. Next comes giving from a crypto wallet, a contacts page and a welcome from the church; the experiments in a network of the community's own begin on the sibling organization. No dates: each step lands in the changelog when it's ready.

Where Psanctuary Church’s GitHub home is headed, in rough order. What’s already done is in the changelog, and what keeps it all running is on the sustainability page. This is also the repository’s ROADMAP.md.

✅ Just landed

  • A full navbar on every page, folding into a Menu on smaller screens, with an icon on every link.
  • One window for every way to reach the church, and one for the member community, with both its front page and its membership plans.
  • A map of every place these pages lead, each with its own picture.
  • A donate page with a thank-you list for those who wish to be named.

🔜 Next

  • Give from a crypto wallet on the donate page: one button for browser wallets, phone wallets and email sign-in. The slot is there now, empty on purpose until it’s ready.
  • A contacts page (CONTACTS.md) for this organization, so the placeholder in the Reach out window can go.
  • A welcome from the church. The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from the church’s co-founders, empty on purpose until they’d like to write them.

🌌 Later, and still being explored

  • A network of our own. A small social network owned by the community, on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where members propose ideas and decide together. Privacy first. The experiments begin on the sibling organization, psanctuary. A direction, not a promise.
