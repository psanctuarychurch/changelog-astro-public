Share one entry, and it arrives with its own spore print

TL;DR: Every changelog entry and library write-up now has its own preview image, so a link shared in Telegram, iMessage or Discord shows that entry's title, summary and who made it, beside a spore print grown just for it.
Read the full story

What you’ll see

Paste the link to any entry or write-up into a chat, and the preview is a card made for it: the title, the one-line summary, the date and who made the change, on violet-black. Each card’s spore print is drawn from the entry’s own address, so no two entries share a print, and the same entry always gets the same one.

Under each entry and write-up there’s a Copy a link button that does the copying for you. The welcome guide mentions it too, in its changelog and library step.

How it works

The cards are drawn once, when the site is built, and published as ordinary images next to the pages. Nothing is generated when someone opens a link, and nothing is tracked.

If a chat shows an old preview

Messengers remember previews. If Telegram still shows an older preview, or none, for a link, send the link to @WebpageBot in Telegram and it fetches a fresh one.
