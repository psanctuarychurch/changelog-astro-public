Where Psanctuary Church's GitHub home is headed, in rough order. What's already done is in the
[changelog](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog), and what keeps it
all running is on the [sustainability](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability)
page. This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`.

## ✅ Just landed

- **A [pledge of support](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/pledge/)** in the church's own words,
  signed with a wallet: free, nothing sent, nothing recorded, no counts.
- **Receipts and taxes** on the [donate page](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/donate/), quoting the
  church's own legal notice and donation page, with receipts on request.
- **One link for the member network**: "Join or sign in on Mighty Networks", for visitors and
  members alike.
- **[CONTACTS.md](https://github.com/psanctuarychurch/.github/blob/main/CONTACTS.md)**: every way to
  reach the church, linked from Reach out. And a full
  [organization profile](https://github.com/psanctuarychurch).
- **Donate and the welcome guide everywhere**, and a bottom strip that shows it scrolls on phones.

## 🔜 Next

- **Give from a crypto wallet** on the donate page: the Connect button is there now, as a preview;
  giving opens once the church designates a treasury address of its own (never a placeholder).
- **Thanks you can claim yourself:** a contributor could add their name to the thank-you list by
  signing with a wallet, with no personal details involved.
- **A welcome from the church.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from the church's
  co-founders, empty on purpose until they'd like to write them.

## 🤝 Stewardship and succession

So these pages outlive any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, owns or administers the
GitHub organization and holds the token that publishes this AstroLog). Details on the
[sustainability](https://psanctuarychurch.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability) page.

- [ ] At least two owners for the GitHub organization, from the church's own people
- [ ] Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not personal tokens
- [ ] A token register: every token and secret, its owner and its expiry date
- [ ] A renewal runbook, with a reminder a month before each expiry
- [ ] Hand-over steps written down: adding an owner, transferring repositories, domains and the wallet-connect project
- [ ] Exports and backups of the repositories and anything outside GitHub, on a schedule
- [ ] Wallet connect: the church's own Reown project (a one-line `org.js` change, `give.reownProjectId`), before any treasury address is published

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **A network of our own.** A small social network owned by the community, on open protocols like
  Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where members propose ideas and decide together. Privacy
  first. The experiments begin on the sibling organization,
  [psanctuary](https://psanctuary.github.io/). A direction, not a promise.
